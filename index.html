You know, I've been sitting with this for a looong time now... this feeling I carry around like a quiet song I never quite get to sing out loud and clear and fully. You call me your friend, and I hold that word like it's the most precious thing I've ever been given. Because it is. And yet, honestly... it's also the hardest.
I have loved you in a way that doesn't fit neatly inside the word friend. I've loved you in the way that makes ordinary days feel extraordinary just because you're in them. In the way that made me notice every small thing about you... the way your face changes when you're excited about something, the way your laugh sounds a little different when you're really happy. I've memorised all of it. I didn't mean to. I just couldn't help it.
But here's what I need you to know on your birthday, more than anything: I am not telling you this to make things complicated or to put anything on you. I'm telling you because today is your day, and you deserve to know that someone in this world sees you completely. Not just the version of you that everyone gets to see. All of you. And finds you breathtaking.
You are my favourite person to talk to at 2am. My first thought when something good happens. The one I want sitting next to me for every quiet moment and every loud one. Whether you call me your friend forever or something else someday, I will be right here, proud of you, rooting for you, loving you in whatever way you'll let me.
I don't wish for you to love me the way I love you. I know that's not mine to ask for, and I've made peace with that. What I wish for you genuinely, with everything I have is a life so full and so beautiful that your heart never has room to feel empty. I wish for you someone who looks at you the way I do when you're not watching. Someone who thinks “god, I'm so lucky!” every single day. You deserve that. You deserve all of it.
Happy Birthday, Saswatha. You are the kind of person people write songs about. And I'll keep this feeling tucked right here, close to my chest... just glad that you exist, and that you chose to keep me near. 🌙

Made with care for you.
